# Hi, I'm Charangan 👋

I lead engineering teams that build and ship enterprise AI systems — the kind that actually run in production, across cloud, on-prem, and air-gapped environments.

I'm an **Engineering Manager at [Iterate.ai](https://www.iterate.ai/)**, where I lead a 12-member cross-functional team building **Generate**, our flagship enterprise AI platform. It runs as multi-tenant SaaS and installs air-gapped into regulated industries — the same system in both places, which is most of what makes it hard. We build it alongside IBM, NetApp, Intel, AMD, and HP.

## What I Work On

- **AI platform engineering** — RAG, multi-agent orchestration (LangGraph), and document intelligence at production scale, reaching 94% extraction accuracy on the documents that break naive RAG
- **Deployment everywhere** — multi-cloud (AWS, IBM Cloud), on-prem, and edge inference with model quantization (GGUF, INT8/FP16) across Intel, AMD, and NVIDIA. Private document search runs entirely local on an Intel AI PC
- **Event-driven architecture** — Kafka, FastAPI, and Kubernetes at 99.8% uptime and 50,000+ requests/second peak, observed through OpenTelemetry and Prometheus/Grafana
- **Security and multi-tenancy** — tenant isolation, SSO identities resolved to POSIX UID/GID so vector search is permission-filtered *before* it runs, and sandboxed execution of model-generated code (gVisor/Kata)
- **Engineering leadership** — hiring, mentorship, roadmap, and partnership engineering. I've grown the team from 6 to 12, including 2 tech leads and 2 project managers, and promoted engineers into senior roles
